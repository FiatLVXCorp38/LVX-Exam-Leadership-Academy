COURSE CATALOG

NYS County Corrections

Five focused preparation paths for entry-level candidates and professionals pursuing advancement in New York State county corrections.

One shared foundation. Five career paths.

Shared foundation: New York State Minimum Standards for Management of County Jails, numbers and timeframes, written material, reading comprehension, grammar, and examination strategy.

Rank-specific preparation: Entry candidates build foundational knowledge. Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain, and Chief candidates apply the standards through progressively broader supervision, operational oversight, administration, and command responsibilities.

Course paths are study organization, not a claim that every title has an identical exam. Always follow the current examination announcement for the exact subject areas.

WRITTEN EXAM TRACK

Preparing Written Material

Paragraph sequencing and accurate information presentation.

Fast paragraph-sequencing method

  1. Find the broad opening sentence.
  2. Pair sentences using pronouns, repeated terms, and transitions.
  3. Follow chronology, cause/effect, or general-to-specific logic.
  4. Find the conclusion or result.
  5. Eliminate choices that start with a sentence depending on something not yet introduced.
