THE LVX DAILY MASTERY METHOD

Repetition is the mother of learning.

Testing is how knowledge becomes mastery. Don't study until you get it right once. Study until you can get it right consistently.

01 / LEARN

Understand the concept

Short lessons explain the rule, the reasoning behind it, and how to apply it.

02 / TEST

Practice every day

Use daily randomized questions to strengthen recall and apply knowledge in new contexts.

03 / REVIEW

Study every mistake

Review incorrect answers with explanations that show why the correct choice is right.

04 / RETEST

Repeat until consistent

Return to weak concepts and test again with a fresh set of questions.

Daily recommendation: Complete a practice session, review every missed question, and revisit weak topics the next day. Progress comes from consistent practice—not one perfect score.

WRITTEN EXAM TRACK

Preparing Written Material

Paragraph sequencing and accurate information presentation.

Fast paragraph-sequencing method

  1. Find the broad opening sentence.
  2. Pair sentences using pronouns, repeated terms, and transitions.
  3. Follow chronology, cause/effect, or general-to-specific logic.
  4. Find the conclusion or result.
  5. Eliminate choices that start with a sentence depending on something not yet introduced.
